One more decision is needed before Stage 1 can compute shares. The numerator of a share is in mL, and adults is a head count. So s_{i,t} = mL_{i,t} / adults_t isn't a share yet. You need one more number: potential mL per adult per month, k, so that M_t = k × adults_t. This is the usual move, like Nevo's "one serving per capita per day" in cereal.
- What k does: it sets the outside share s_0, which enters both sides of eq. 7. So it moves α̂ and σ̂ somewhat, though usually not by much.
- How to pick it: take a round number based on a regular vaper's monthly mL, then rerun Stage 1 with k halved and doubled as a robustness check.
- What to expect: RMS only tracks part of retail, and most adults don't vape. So s_0 will be close to 1 for any reasonable k, which is fine for the nested logit.
You can pick k now, or I can take a placeholder and rerun with other values later.
There's also a unit issue with the tax data. Many state e-cig taxes aren't per mL:

The standard fix is a price that's fixed in advance, such as a national average $/mL from a base year.
