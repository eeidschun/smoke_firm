# Dynamic entry/exit preparation

Run from the repository root. No canonical panel or static recovery input is modified.

1. `1_explore_transition_bins.R`: compare marginal and joint quantile/equal-width grids; output `output/dynamic_entry_exit/transition_bins_2026-11-03/`.
2. `2_estimate_transition_matrices.R`: estimate separate pooled, time-homogeneous three-state nicotine and xi Markov matrices under maintained independence. Use within-marginal-bin medians for both variables. Outputs, exact counts, assigned observations, source hash and assumptions: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/independent_transitions_2026-11-03/`. `transition_inputs.rds` contains compact numerical inputs, not fitted model objects.

The same 531 adjacent within-brand pairs are used for each matrix. Counts are independently tabulated and reconciled to the exploratory exercise; probability sums and the implied product matrix indexing are checked. No smoothing is applied. Samples reflect the annual incumbent roster, so economic continuation/exit and selection require further work. Fringe evolution, cross-firm shock dependence, future costs, and entry/exit primitives remain undecided. No Bellman problem is solved by these scripts.

3. `3_compare_nicotine_grids.R`: sensitivity comparison of baseline terciles, higher cutoffs (15/30 and 20/30), quartiles and quintiles. Reports occupancy, crossings, per-firm counts, median representation error and matrices; produces a five-page plot booklet. No alternative overwrites the baseline. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/nicotine_grid_comparison_2026-11-03/`.

4. `4_audit_recovered_marginal_costs.R`: exploratory four-page cost time-series and nicotine audit, firm summaries, descriptive variance decompositions, and overlapping monthly-change correlations. Output: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/marginal_cost_audit_2026-11-03/`. No cost rule is selected and meeting notes are not modified.

5. `5_assign_constant_marginal_costs.R`: selected time-invariant incumbent costs are unweighted within-firm medians across observed roster months. Full-precision CSV and compact inputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/constant_marginal_costs_2026-11-03/`. Entrant cost remains unspecified. Source recovery and historical pricing audits are unchanged.

6. `6_assign_entrant_marginal_cost.R`: selected entrant cost is the pooled liquid-volume-weighted mean recovered MC over the first twelve calendar incumbent months of brands first observed as incumbents after sample start. Outputs in `constant_marginal_costs_2026-11-03/` include firm contributions, calibration observations and separate `entrant_cost_inputs.rds`; these supplement the incumbent-only inputs. It is a successful-expansion benchmark, not an upper bound or verified economic-entry technology.

7. `7_assign_constant_fringe_utility.R`: initial passive-fringe total utility is the unweighted median of 132 monthly calibrated utilities. CSV and compact RDS inputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/constant_fringe_2026-11-03/`. Fringe share remains endogenous to incumbent competition; original monthly recovery is unchanged.

8. `8_assign_constant_market_size.R`: initial stationary market size is final-sample December 2023 M from the selected demand specification, held fixed at every future horizon. CSV and compact RDS inputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/constant_market_size_2026-11-03/`. Historical-date-specific stationary solves remain unimplemented; no population forecasts or historical input changes.

9. `9_calibrate_entrant_quality.R`: volume-weighted joint initial nicotine/xi distribution using the same five firms and 60 initial-year observations as entrant MC. Existing bins and median representatives retained; subsequent independent marginal transitions unchanged. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/entrant_quality_2026-11-04/`. Initial quality is revealed after sunk-cost commitment and used directly at next-month activation.

10. `10_count_top5_admission_roster.R`: monthly liquid-volume top-five admission with permanent retention after admission, compared with removal after last observed positive-volume sale (a proxy, not verified economic shutdown). Main rankings match demand's >=3-store UPC-month filters; all-positive-sales sensitivity included. Brand-month counts, admissions, annual ranges, ranks and plots: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/top5_admission_roster_2026-11-04/`. No dynamic model or meeting notes changed.

11. `11_initialize_latest_roster.R`: select latest demand incumbent roster (December 2023 JUUL/NJOY/VUSE), map quality/xi to existing bin medians, attach historical median costs, and record two vacancies under cap five. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/initial_roster_2026-11-04/`. No dynamic equilibrium or trial payoffs computed.

12. `12_audit_entry_exit_candidates.R`: summarize nine historical demand-incumbent brands' full positive-volume sales histories, incumbent spells, gaps and censoring. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/entry_exit_candidates_2026-11-04/`. First/last scanner sales are candidate evidence only; no verified economic-entry or shutdown dates assigned. Requires script 10 outputs.

13. `13_prepare_historical_market_sizes.R`: selected historical M path (132 months, eleven annual values), with each current value perceived as permanent by firms. Inputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/historical_market_sizes_2026-11-04/`. Future solver must solve stationary policies at each M per trial parameter vector, then apply them along the historical path. No dynamic solve implemented here.

14. `14_check_event_sequence_cap.R`: replay candidate scanner entries/shutdowns among nine ever-incumbent brands, conditioned on the January 2013 four-firm demand roster. Checks entry at the preceding month's decision date against starting-roster capacity; reports unrestricted candidate history and capped replay separately. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/event_cap_check_2026-11-04/`. Exposes support conflicts; does not silently select an estimation sample or treat initial fringe promotions as industry births.

15. `15_test_low_sales_exit_rules.R`: sensitivity of entry capacity to absolute monthly mL thresholds 0–100,000. Compares terminal last-above-threshold exit with first three consecutive low-sales months; flags later rebounds and reports pre-JUUL initial-incumbent sales. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/low_sales_exit_sensitivity_2026-11-04/`. No selected event definition changed.

16. `16_select_focal_brands.R`: compare cumulative-volume top five union annual leaders for 2013–2023. Demand-eligible and all-positive-sales universes reported separately. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/focal_brand_selection_2026-11-05/`. Six brands selected under the proposed rule; no dynamic roster or fringe inputs replaced.

17. `17_rebuild_fixed_focal_supply.R`: rebuild eligible focal histories for the selected five identities and residual fringe including LOGIC, retaining demand coefficients. Saves new median fringe utility, original constant cost assignments, initial roster, coverage/bin-range flags and 264 manual observed-price checks. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/fixed_focal_supply_2026-11-05/`. Added MISTIC negative monthly MCs are unmodified; missing eligible cells and scanner/eligible entry-date differences are not imputed. No dynamic equilibrium solved.

18. `18_benchmark_variable_profits.R`: initial binned BLU/MISTIC/NJOY market plus nine-state JUUL/VUSE entry scenarios (including joint entry), using retained brand costs and revised fringe. Runs 400 manual pricing solves across 2013/2023 M and unilateral checks; reports weighted variable profits, unselected trial scales, and an initial-assumptions attribution table. Outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/benchmark_profits_2026-11-05/`. The fixed historical-median fringe severely reduces initial profits; trial grids are diagnostics, not adopted estimates or continuation values.

19. `19_assign_january_fringe_benchmark.R`: selected baseline is January 2013 fixed-focal total fringe utility (-7.371012...), held constant; original median inputs preserved. Saved selected input bundle: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/january_fringe_benchmark_2026-11-06/`. Run script 18 with `--january-fringe` to recompute profit scenarios in `benchmark_profits_january_fringe_2026-11-06/`; without the flag it reproduces the earlier median sensitivity case. Section 24 of meeting notes records the large profit contrast.

## First manual dynamic trial (November 6, 2026)

20. `20_dynamic_solver_functions.R`: manual joint value/policy iteration, independent transition contractions, analytic expected-max normal scrap and exponential sunk-cost formulas. Each active initial brand has 9 quality states plus absorbing shutdown. JUUL/VUSE additionally have a not-yet-entered state. Total: 121,000 states. A not-yet-entered named candidate retains the option to wait and reconsider next month, so its entry threshold is discounted entry value minus discounted waiting value. This differs from an anonymous one-period potential entrant with zero non-entry payoff.
21. `21_prepare_dynamic_payoffs.R`: manual vectorized static markup iteration over all 100,000 active-roster/quality configurations. January 2013 M/fringe, brand-specific median costs. Scalar independent-start and unilateral checks for 45 selected nonempty cases. Saves compact static payoff cache and validation results.
22. `22_validate_dynamic_functions.R`: exact small-state next-state enumeration, quadrature checks of expected-max formulas, one-firm fixed point and explicit iteration-budget failure.
23. `23_run_dynamic_trial.R`: zero-value initial start; FC $100,000/month, mean sunk entry cost $100m, scrap SD $5m and mean zero, annual discount .95 converted monthly. Joint damping .7, scaled Bellman tolerance 1e-6 (absolute floor $1), policy tolerance 1e-7. Checkpoints are monitoring outputs, not auto-resumed states. No historical fit or parameter estimation.
24. `24_audit_dynamic_trial.R`: independently recompute all-state Bellman/policy residuals and explicitly enumerate next-state probabilities at selected full-model states; generate convergence plot.
25. `25_alternative_dynamic_start.R`: negative $1m value start and .5 continuation/entry probabilities, with undamped joint updates, as a second-start robustness check. Convergence is reported rather than assumed.

Trial outputs: `output/dynamic_entry_exit/manual_dynamic_trial_2026-11-06/`. Run scripts 21 and 22 before 23; script 24 requires a completed converged 23 output. Quality kernels and selected G_e are held fixed, with independent entrant initial-state draws across brands. Policies/value iteration is an equilibrium fixed point and is not guaranteed to contract; a converged solution does not prove uniqueness or identify payoff parameters. The terminal no-active-firm market has only passive-fringe demand. Original data, reconstructed demand estimates and prior static audits are preserved.

26. `26_simulate_stationary_trial.R`: 5,000 stationary 120-month paths with terminal-value bootstrap; compare simulated discounted cash flows with initial values and enforce no reentry/cap invariants.
27. `27_accelerate_dynamic_trial.R`: resume saved monitoring checkpoint with manual depth-four residual-minimizing value extrapolation and damped policy updates. Accept only residual-safeguarded candidates. `--alternative` uses the negative-value/.5-policy checkpoint. The original unaccelerated runs were stopped at 900 and 650 updates; those checkpoints are not converged solutions. Run 24 and 26 after the accelerated reference finishes.

28. `28_compare_dynamic_starts.R`: independently check alternative final residuals and compare values/probabilities across the two converged runs.

29. `29_plot_entry_exit_diagnostics.R`: simulate 10,000 stationary paths over 132 months; compare first-entry CDFs, initial-incumbent survival, brand activity and active-firm counts with full-scanner first/last-sales event proxies. Outputs in `manual_dynamic_trial_2026-11-06/entry_exit_diagnostics/`. Holds January 2013 M/fringe fixed, so this is a trial diagnostic, not historical parameter estimation.

30. `30_coordinate_search.R`: up to two coarse coordinate passes over FC {0,100k,300k}, exponential mean entry {30m,100m,300m}, normal scrap SD {1m,5m,15m}. Re-solves dynamic game for each candidate; loss averages entry-CDF, initial-survival, and count/5 MSEs over 132 months using 2,000 common-random-number paths. Failed solves cannot be selected. Saves selected value/policy state and compact candidate scores in `coordinate_search_2026-11-06/`; January 2013 M/fringe remain fixed.
31. `31_report_coordinate_search.R`: comparison PDF, candidate rankings and findings after script 30 completes. This is provisional calibration, not parameter estimation or a global-optimum claim.
