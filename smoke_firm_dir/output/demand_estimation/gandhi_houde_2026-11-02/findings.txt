Replacing annual count by these nicotine differentiation instruments does not produce admissible sigma in the closest chosen-specification comparison. Adding local counts (or local+quadratic) to the baseline retains admissible sigma but moves it nearer one. No automatic improvement or instrument-validity claim follows.

Using nicotine alone without tax or annual count is extremely weak: conditional F approximately 1.2e-7 for the B both-measures design, enormous coefficient estimates, and failed independent numerical agreement. These six market/control fits are logged as unstable, not reported as trustworthy point estimates.

Size instruments require full rival-size coverage, so all 2018 months are excluded (468 observations / 120 months). Their baselines refitted on exactly the same sample have no admissible sigma. Some size-based quadratic sets produce admissible estimates, but cannot be compared to full-sample baseline without the matched controls/sample results. See matched_comparison.csv.

All six full-coverage nicotine baselines reproduce prior saved coefficients and SEs. Retained estimates pass independent explicit QR checks and conditional-F sandwich checks. Exogeneity of nicotine, size, and assortment is maintained, not established; GH-style instruments do not solve endogenous positioning or tax sales-weight endogeneity by construction. No firm-ownership decomposition beyond one independent brand/product, no fringe differentiation characteristics, no interactions, no predicted-price instruments, no endogenous-quality extension in this first pass.
